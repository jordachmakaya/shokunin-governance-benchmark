# Experimental principles

Status: candidate for G0 review.

1. External truth outranks agent self-certification.
2. One bounded intervention is varied at a time.
3. Measurement instrumentation is identical across arms.
4. Development governance never enters experimental workspaces.
5. Raw evidence is immutable; aggregates are generated.
6. Negative, mixed, and insufficient results remain valid outcomes.
7. A primitive is promoted only after confirmatory evidence across declared conditions.
8. Public claims never exceed the evidence that can be audited or reproduced.
